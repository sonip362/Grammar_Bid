The 5-Phase Auction Loop

Every match consists of 5 competitive rounds with dynamic AI sentences.

Phase 1 ⏱️ 30 Seconds

🔍 Inspection

Analyze the candidate sentence on the block. Players can spend $300 Vault Cash to buy a hint, or activate the 💡 Double Hint card for detailed rule explanations.

Phase 2 ⏱️ 30 Seconds

💰 Bidding

Place live cash bids to acquire the lot. Bids must exceed current top bid by +$100. Bids in the final 3s trigger Anti-Sniping (+2s timer). Activate ⚡ Bid Boost, 🛡️ Bid Shield, or 💰 Cashback.

Phase 3 ⏱️ 10 Seconds

📊 Result Reveal

The gavel falls! Winning a Correct sentence awards bid amount cash. Winning an Incorrect sentence loses the bid amount cash (unless protected by Bid Shield/Cashback).

Phase 4 ⏱️ 30 Seconds

✏️ Bonus Correction

If sentence was flawed, all players rush to fix the phrase. First correct fix wins +$500 bonus & XP. Activate 🔄 Second Chance directly inside the modal for a 2nd attempt!

Phase 5 ⏱️ 8 Seconds

📖 Solution & Ranks Reveal

Review the official solution, comprehensive grammar rule breakdown, player submission results, and updated XP ranks before advancing to the next round.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I join a private room with a 4-digit code? ▼

On the homepage header, type the 4-digit room code into the "Enter Room Code..." box and click Join! Or ask your friend for their direct private room link.

How do I earn more Gold Tokens? ▼

Gold Tokens are earned through Daily Reward claims, leveling up your XP Rank (+20 Tokens per rank), and newly registered accounts automatically receive a 50 Token Signup Bonus!

How do Anti-Sniping bids work? ▼

If any player places a valid bid in the final 3 seconds of the Bidding Phase, the round timer automatically extends by +2 seconds to ensure everyone has a fair chance to respond.

What happens if I use Second Chance in the correction modal? ▼

Activating Second Chance inside the Correction Modal grants you 1 additional correction attempt during that round if your first correction attempt turns out to be incorrect.
